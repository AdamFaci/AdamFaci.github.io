With Léa Maronet.

Corpora of ancient Indian bas-reliefs pose major challenges for automatic image processing, particularly for recognising iconographic motifs and describing them finely. The scarcity of available annotations and the limited size of the corpora constrain models to generic classifications, impoverishing iconographic analysis.

To move past these limits, this talk proposes a hybrid approach crossing automatic motif segmentation with semantic enrichment. The latter combines two methods: unsupervised clustering interpreted through expert knowledge of the corpus, and supervised multi-class classification using fine-tuned multimodal models — both enriched through ontologies and a knowledge graph. The work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and human interpretation, offering an alternative to plain labels in order to produce richer and more nuanced descriptions of motifs. The aim is to move beyond the formalist and positivist readings imposed by general-purpose models, and to open new perspectives for iconographic analysis.
