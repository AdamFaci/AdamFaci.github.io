With Julien Schuh. If an agent is given an explicit symbolic profile — a set of expectations, a genre competence, a reading protocol — its reading of a corpus becomes a position that can be compared with another agent's. The talk presents such simulated readers over a large literary corpus, and what their disagreements reveal.
