With Alice Breton, Francesca Frontini, Pascal Cuxac, Alessio Miaschi, Nicolas Larrousse and Stéphane Pouyllau.

The final goal is to build an AI agent that will help in retrieving relevant documents, for a future version of Isidore (Isidore 2030), as well as helping in performing research tasks: currently mainly question answering and discussion with documents, and the process of state-of-the-art construction and update according to different criteria, such as originality and relevance.
