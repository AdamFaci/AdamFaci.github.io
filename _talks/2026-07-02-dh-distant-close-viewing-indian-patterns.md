With Léa Maronet.

This study proposes a frugal computer-vision workflow for analysing ancient Indian patterns by combining clustering, multimodality, and expert validation. It demonstrates that small-scale heritage datasets can support fine-grained iconographic analysis, showing how lightweight methods, grounded in scholarly engagement, yield precise and interpretable results beyond generic automated classifications.
