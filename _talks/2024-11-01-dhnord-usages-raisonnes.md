With Stéphane Pouyllau and Antoine Silvestre de Sacy. From the work of the HN Lab to the ISIDORE 2030 programme: a case for *reasoned* uses of AI in the social sciences and humanities: choosing the method for the research question, documenting what the tool did, and recognising the cases where AI is not the right answer.
