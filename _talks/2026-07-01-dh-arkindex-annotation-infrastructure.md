With Alice Truc, Léa Maronet, Marion Charpier, Julien Schuh and Christopher Kermorvant.

This article reflects on annotation practices for small visual corpora. It reports on workshops and annotation formats aimed at building durable interpretive frameworks. The goal is to adapt computer-vision AI methods to humanities and social-science domains through fine-grained, situated annotations that support meaningful, domain-sensitive analysis.
