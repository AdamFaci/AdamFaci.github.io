A series of guest lectures given in 2024 and 2025 in the course *Quand l'IA se met à écrire* ("When AI starts writing"), based on work carried out at the HN Lab with Antoine Silvestre de Sacy, Léa Maronet, Émile Provendier and Stéphane Pouyllau.

Sessions
======

**Controlled use of LLMs through RAG** (12 November 2024) — What a large language model is, as a statistical function trained on pre-training tasks such as next-word or masked-word prediction; why models are not only generative and not only chatbots; and how retrieval-augmented generation restores a measure of control: keeping the generation anchored to a specific corpus, exposing the context that produced an answer, and improving relevance and interpretability while remaining computationally frugal.

**A photojournalistic project with SAM** (25 November 2025) — Tokenisation, word embeddings and attention as the working parts of a transformer, then their vision counterparts: segmentation and clustering of a photojournalistic corpus with Segment Anything.

**Towards an interdisciplinary epistemology** (2 December 2025) — Methods still to be invented: appropriating the available technologies rather than accepting their intended uses; distinguishing the stages of a project (collection, pre-processing, analysis, presentation); keeping symbolic and neural AI apart rather than blending them; and comparing and combining systems — including combining models, as in a DINO + SAM2 pipeline applied to the Élie Kagan photographic collection.
