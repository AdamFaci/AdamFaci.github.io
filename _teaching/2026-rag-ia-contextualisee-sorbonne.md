Session of the *Interprétations artificielles* seminar on retrieval-augmented generation as a way of keeping interpretation contestable.

The session starts from the limits of large language models — black boxes whose training is undocumented, hallucinations that are fluent by construction, no explanation of an output, no control over behaviour, high variability from small input changes, and a credibility that is itself misleading — and presents RAG as the response: keeping control of the model, contextualising queries on a specific corpus, countering hallucination by exposing the retrieved context that produced a generation, and improving relevance, coherence, explainability and interpretability, at a low computational cost.
