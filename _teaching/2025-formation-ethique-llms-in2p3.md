Training session on the limits and effects of generative AI: hallucinations, ethical implications, and the impact of these systems on research practice.

The session treats hallucination not as an anomaly to be patched but as a predictable consequence of how generation works (no step in the computation checks a referent), and moves from there to the questions a research team has to settle before using such a system: what the model was trained on, what can be asserted from its output, and what has to be documented for the result to be defensible.
