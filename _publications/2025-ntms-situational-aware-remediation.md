As cyberattacks become increasingly sophisticated, structured remediation is essential to contain threats effectively. While existing semantic resources, such as cybersecurity vocabularies and ontologies, provide a foundation for remediation strategies, their practical application remains challenging due to the fragmentation of cybersecurity tools and the difficulty of integrating these resources into operational workflows.

After exploring the state of the art relying on cybersecurity incident remediation and highlighting their drawbacks, we suggest through this paper to go towards a situational awareness-based remediation process. This process is roughly outlined in this paper. We also highlight the knowledge to be formalized and to be provided to the process in the perspective of its automation.
