A model can produce fluent definitions of two concepts while being inconsistent about which subsumes the other. Lattices make that failure measurable: they impose order-theoretic constraints — reflexivity, transitivity, incomparability — that a competent representation must satisfy.

This work builds a diagnostic from those constraints and then uses them as a training signal, connecting the conceptual-graph tradition of my doctoral work to current language models.
