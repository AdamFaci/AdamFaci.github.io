Reasoning modes are often assumed to make model judgements more defensible, and therefore fairer. This work tests that assumption on generated peer review, where the manipulated variables — author name, gender, institutional prestige — should be irrelevant to the verdict by construction.

The aim is to disaggregate the effect: which components of the review (score, tone, requested revisions, confidence) remain sensitive to status once the model reasons explicitly.
