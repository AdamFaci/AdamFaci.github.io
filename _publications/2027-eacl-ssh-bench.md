Existing benchmarks tell us little about whether a model is usable for humanities research: they reward factual recall in English, while SSH scholarship is multilingual, argumentative, and organised around contested concepts.

SSH-Bench is a French–English multitask benchmark built on scholarly SSH literature, designed so that a high score requires handling disciplinary vocabulary, citation context and argumentative structure rather than surface retrieval.
