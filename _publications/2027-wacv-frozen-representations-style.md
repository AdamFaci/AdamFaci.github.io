Using a frozen foundation-model encoder to compare artworks is cheap and increasingly standard. It is also hard to validate: apparent stylistic proximity in embedding space may reflect lighting, degradation, photographic campaign or support rather than transmission between workshops.

This paper sets out a validation protocol for such representations under confounding, and applies it to a case where the art-historical hypothesis is transmission rather than resemblance.
