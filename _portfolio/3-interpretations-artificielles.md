*Interprétations artificielles* is a seminar run with Julien Schuh on what it means for a machine to interpret a text, and on what alternative, machine-assisted interpretations do to the humanities disciplines that adopt them.

I took part in 2025 and co-organise the seminar in 2026. An edited volume drawing on the sessions is in preparation.
