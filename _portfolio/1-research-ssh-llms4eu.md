I lead the *ReSearch SSH* use case of the European project **LLMs4EU**, with Alice Breton, Stéphane Pouyllau, Nicolas Larrousse, Francesca Frontini, Alessio Miaschi and Pascal Cuxac.

The objective is a research assistant that SSH scholars can actually rely on: multilingual, because SSH scholarship is not written in English; interpretable, because a claim about a corpus has to be traceable to the passages that support it; and adapted to scholarly literature rather than to general web text.

The work combines domain adaptation on multilingual scholarly corpora with knowledge graphs that supply the disciplinary structure a corpus alone leaves implicit. The approach was presented at the [LLMs4SSH workshop at LREC 2026](/publications/2026-lrec-llms4ssh-knowledge-graphs/) and as a [poster at ALT-EDIC Week 2026](/talks/2026-06-15-alt-edic-domain-adaptive-llm).

The target application is a future version of [Isidore](https://isidore.science/) (Isidore 2030): an agent that retrieves relevant documents and assists with research tasks: question answering over documents, and the construction and updating of a state of the art according to criteria such as originality and relevance.
