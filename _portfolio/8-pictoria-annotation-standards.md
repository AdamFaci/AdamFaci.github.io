Since November 2024 I co-direct a working group of the PictorIA consortium on **image annotation standards in SSH**, with Emmanuel Château-Dutier, Léa Maronet and Alice Truc.

Annotation is where a research question becomes data, and it is precisely the step that current infrastructures standardise least. The group works on conventions that are usable across projects without flattening the interpretive distinctions each project depends on — including for small, interpretive datasets, where the label scheme is itself part of the research output.
