With Émile Provendier. Before late 2022, artificial intelligence appeared in the French press mainly as invisible automation: recommendation, sorting, surveillance. The conversational interface changed the object of the discourse, and with it the actors, the metaphors and the anxieties.

The project analyses a three-year Europresse corpus on generative AI in France, then compares it across contexts to distinguish what is specific to the French debate from what belongs to the technology's global reception.
