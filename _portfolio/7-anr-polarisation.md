Work carried out for the ANR Polarisation project (2023–2025) with Camille Bouzereau and Sybila Guéneau, on a corpus of French crime fiction. A book is in preparation.

Three strands:

* **Contrastive corpus description through pattern analysis** — using recurrent motifs to characterise sub-corpora against one another rather than in isolation.
* **Violent motifs** — their distribution and their forms across the corpus.
* **The structure of opening sentences** — presented at *Bases Corpus Langage* (Université Côte d'Azur), 2024.

A related study of gender markers in crime-fiction dialogue (1945–1989), with Camille Bouzereau and D. Longrée, was presented at the *Journées Linguistique de Corpus*.
