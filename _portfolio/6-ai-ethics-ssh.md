An ongoing strand of work on the evaluation of discriminatory bias in generative language models, and on turning that evaluation into guidelines that SSH communities can apply.

It has two sides. The measurement side asks how bias survives the mitigations that are supposed to remove it, for instance whether explicit reasoning modes actually neutralise the effect of an author's gender or institutional prestige on a generated peer review, or merely relocate it. The prescriptive side turns those findings into recommendations usable by laboratories and infrastructures, and feeds directly into the [training sessions](/teaching/) I run for CNRS and Huma-Num.
