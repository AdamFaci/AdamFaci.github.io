Following a postdoctoral position at CNAM in 2023 on ontologies for cybersecurity, I have continued to work on the operationalisation of these ontologies for incident response, and co-supervised the doctoral work of Rayan Kanawati with Nadira Lammari and Nada Mimouni (2024–2025).

The problem is that remediation guidance is written independently of the systems it applies to. Modelling that context explicitly makes it possible to filter and rank candidate remediations by applicability — see [*Towards a Situational Aware-Based Cybersecurity Remediation*](/publications/2025-ntms-situational-aware-remediation/) (NTMS 2025).
